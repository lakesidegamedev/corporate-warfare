# Corporate Warfare

A business strategy game in which an investor builds and contests corporate groups
within a regional economy.

## Language

**Investor**: A persistent person or institutional owner holding cash and company shares.
The player remains this investor after losing a company.

**Firm**: A legal company with its own assets, liabilities, owners and governance.
_Avoid_: Sector, facility, player account.

**Holding company**: A firm whose strategy includes controlling other firms.

**Subsidiary**: A firm controlled through another firm's voting rights. Subsidiary status
is different from a non-controlling investment.

**Group**: A controlling firm and its controlled subsidiaries, viewed together.

**Facility**: An operating site belonging to a firm in one region.

**Region**: The shared labour and destination demand market used to locate facilities.

**Product**: A traded good or service with declared quantity units and market rules.

**Control**: Authority established by voting rights and governance rules, distinct from
economic ownership.

**Career**: The investor's continuous play history across controlled and lost companies.

**Scenario**: A starting world and explicit objectives or scoring horizon.

**Cast**: The twelve fictional people who hold named posts: central bank chair, party leaders, governors and rival chief executives. A person is content, with a home scenario, background, temperament and the posts they can hold. A person holds one post at a time.

**Temperament**: How a rival's chief executive tends to play: integrator, raider, financier, speculator, builder or cautious.

**Gateway**: An outside-economy trading source or destination with finite priced capacity.
