## Copy

App name: Corporate Warfare

Short description (80 characters at most; this one is 74):

> Build a business empire on a living 3D map. Offline, no ads, no purchases.

Full description:

> Run a company in a living economy and grow it into a business empire.
>
> Corporate Warfare is an offline corporate strategy game. Take over an established company or
> found your own, then build plants, set prices, hire, borrow and expand across a real map of the
> Great Lakes, Texas, Silicon Valley or the Ruhr. Every market trades, every price moves, and
> every rival plays by the same rules you do.
>
> - Build and run divisions across fifteen industries, from farms and refineries to banks and
>   software, with real supply chains between them.
> - Watch the map come alive: raised regions that show where you are strong, your plants as
>   buildings, freight on the lanes, traffic on the highways and real weather drifting overhead.
> - Trade on the exchange: stocks with a market index, commodities with their own price history.
> - Fight rivals who undercut, invade and raid, or take them over.
> - Ride the business cycle through booms, busts and historical commodity shocks.
> - Play a five-year scored career, or keep going as long as you like.
>
> No account, no ads, no in-app purchases. The game plays fully offline. An anonymous market
> summary is shared every five game weeks to balance the economy, and can be switched off in
> Settings.
