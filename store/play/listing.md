> Run a company in a living economy and try to finish a career as the richest, the biggest or the last one standing.
>
> Corporate Warfare is an offline business strategy game for people who like tycoon games with real systems under them. Take over an established company or found your own, then build plants, set prices, hire, borrow and expand across a real map of the Great Lakes, Texas, Silicon Valley or the Ruhr. Every market trades, every price moves, and every rival plays by the same rules you do.
>
> CAREERS WITH AN ENDING
> - Pick a start: the helm of an established company, or a founder with a name and some capital.
> - Pick a goal: richest, biggest, dominant or survivor.
> - Play five, ten or twenty years for a bronze, silver or gold finish. Or start with no goal and keep going as a sandbox.
>
> RIVALS THAT BUILD AND RAID
> - Rival companies build plants, close the ones that lose money and compete for room to grow. The Openings board shows where a new plant would pay.
> - Raids are announced four weeks ahead, so you can raise your marketing and defend.
> - A failed company's plants go to a four week auction. Bid for them, or list your own.
>
> MARKETS THAT MOVE
> - Fifteen industries, from farms and refineries to banks and software, with real supply chains between them.
> - Stocks and commodities, each with its own price history.
> - World prices have weather, ports have limits, and demand follows the business cycle through booms, busts and historical commodity shocks.
> - Electricity is shared across a whole grid, not one county.
>
> DENSE TABLES, SAVED VIEWS
> - Sort, filter and compare companies, goods, plants and regions in tables built for people who like numbers.
> - Save the views you use every week and open them again in one tap.
>
> A MAP YOU CAN READ
> - Your plants stand on the map as buildings, with freight on the lanes, traffic on the highways, smoke from running plants, weather overhead and lights at night.
> - Double-tap to zoom in on a region and see the plants up close.
>
