Corporate Warfare is a premium offline economic simulation. Play the Great Lakes in 2000,
Texas in 1981, Silicon Valley in 1995 or the Ruhr and Rhine in 1991. Take the helm of an
established company or found your own: build plants, set prices, hire and bargain with workers,
trade shares, contest boards and takeovers, borrow, insure, lobby and survive bankruptcy and
raids from rivals who play by the same rules. Play a career for a bronze, silver or gold medal
over five, ten or twenty years, or keep going in an open-ended sandbox. No account, no ads, no
mandatory network connection.
