Corporate Warfare is a premium offline business simulation set in the Great Lakes corridor at
the start of the 2000s. You are an investor, not a company: build a holding group, fund
facilities, hire and bargain with workers, trade shares, contest boards and takeovers, borrow,
insure, lobby and survive bankruptcy. Play the five-year scored scenario or continue the same
career in an open-ended sandbox. No account, no ads, no mandatory network connection.
