Corporate Warfare is a premium offline economic simulation. The first scenario opens in the
Great Lakes in 2000, with more countries and scenarios to come. You are an investor, not a
company: build a holding group, fund facilities, hire and bargain with workers, trade shares,
contest boards and takeovers, borrow, insure, lobby and survive bankruptcy. Play the five-year
scored scenario or continue the same career in an open-ended sandbox. No account, no ads, no
mandatory network connection.
