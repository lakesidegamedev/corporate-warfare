# Privacy policy (draft for owner review)

Corporate Warfare is a single-player game that plays offline.

- It does not create accounts, collect personal data, or track you across apps or websites.
- Anonymous market analytics: every five game weeks the game sends a summary of its simulated
  economy (what was made, sold, imported, exported and held, at what prices) and your company's
  in-game results to Lakeside Games, with a random install id. It contains no names, account,
  contact or device identifiers and is used only to balance the game. It is on by default and
  can be switched off under Portfolio, Settings. App Store privacy answer: Gameplay Content,
  used for Analytics, not linked to you, not used for tracking.
- Saves, settings and onboarding completion stay on your device. You may export a save or a
  diagnostic bundle from the Saves screen; sharing happens only when you choose to share it.
- The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
- Deleting the app deletes its local saves. Export a save first if you want a backup.
