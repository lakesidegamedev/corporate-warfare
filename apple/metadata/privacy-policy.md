# Privacy policy (draft for owner review)

Corporate Warfare is an offline single-player game.

- It does not create accounts, collect personal data, track you across apps or websites, or send
  gameplay data anywhere.
- Saves, settings and onboarding completion stay on your device. You may export a save or a
  diagnostic bundle from the Saves screen; sharing happens only when you choose to share it.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
- Deleting the app deletes its local saves. Export a save first if you want a backup.

Owner action before submission: publish this text at a stable public URL and add the review
contact. No contact or URL is invented here.
