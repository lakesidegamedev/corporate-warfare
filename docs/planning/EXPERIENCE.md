# Player experience and acceptance routes

## Information hierarchy

The investor portfolio sits above firms. One selected group is the default operating
context; every action shows the legal firm spending money or accepting liability.
Group views distinguish consolidated figures from parent-only cash and minority-owned
assets. Currency, scale, period, nominal basis and forecast assumptions are visible.

Navigation is proposed as Portfolio, Company, Regions, Markets and Inbox, with contextual
Finance and Ownership drill-downs. Final tab choices follow phone usability work, not
the historical plan. Preserve selected entity and back position across drill-downs.
Avoid wide desktop tables squeezed onto a phone. Provide searchable lists, filters,
comparison cards and tappable explanation rows.

## Screen contracts

| Surface | Must answer | Required actions / states |
|---|---|---|

| Group dashboard | Why did value/cash change and what needs attention? | Cash/profit bridge, alerts, upcoming maturities, subsidiary contribution |
| Facility | What limits output and delivered margin? | Staff/input/demand bottlenecks, build/retool/mothball, stock, prices, wage policy |
| Region/map | Where should I expand? | Labour, costs, product demand, supplier access, freight, comparison of candidates |
| Product market | Can I sell profitably here? | Actual demand/sales, delivered costs, rivals, price/quality, contracts |
| Finance | Can I fund this without losing control? | Statements, cash forecast, debt schedules, loans, bonds, equity, payout preview |
| Ownership/board | Who controls this firm? | Cap table/votes, board, CEO appointment, IPO, buyback, issuance, takeover state |
| Subsidiaries | Is the group better together? | Group tree, authority, internal loans/trade, consolidation, disposal |
| Rivals/deals | What can I learn or acquire? | Public/private information, diligence, offer/tender, remedies and expiry |

## Decision interaction contract

Every material command shows actor, current and projected cash, affected capacity,
lead time, risks, authority requirements and confirmation. Projections are labelled and
not guaranteed sales. Disabling a control includes a reason and route to resolve it.
Reject stale quotes without closing the form or losing the entered intent. Avoid modal
chains for routine recurring policies; batch actions preview totals and partial rejection
rules. Group-wide policies cannot raid minority subsidiary cash without authority.

Pause, step and run speeds have clear state. Fast-forward stops for critical offers,
insolvency, tutorial choices and reached score horizon. No forced action every week;
quiet weeks are useful when a policy is doing its job. Measure meaningful decisions and
explanation comprehension in playtests instead of chasing an arbitrary action count.

## Onboarding route

1. Inherit a small viable-but-constrained firm. Read cash, profit and the first bottleneck.
2. Inspect one facility and region; make a price or staffing decision with a preview.
3. Advance, see a reconciled outcome, open the explanation for the change.
4. Solve a supply constraint with a contract or supplier acquisition.
5. Finance expansion, then read the effect of principal vs interest and dilution.
6. Encounter a rival, a worker negotiation and a governance/control consequence.
7. Exit guidance into the full sandbox with help available in context.

Every step uses real game commands and rules. No tutorial-only free money or exempt AI.
It is possible to resume, skip or restart guidance without corrupting the career.

## Accessibility and polish

Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver names and chart summaries, sufficient contrast,
non-colour status cues, reduced motion and reachable primary actions. Money and long
firm/region names fit narrow screens. No hidden drag-only controls. Screens remain
usable with large text and an onscreen keyboard. Landscape/iPad layouts can add columns
without changing the underlying decisions. Haptics and sound follow settings.

No network is needed after installation. Airplane-mode new game, resume, export and
complete play are release tests. Show no login or unavailable-online banners. About
includes data vintage, estimates, licenses, privacy and feedback route.

## Human acceptance routes

- Newcomer completes onboarding and can explain one profit/cash difference.
- Operator builds a two-region production/distribution chain and identifies freight cost.
- Investor creates a parent/subsidiary structure, issues shares and observes dilution.
- Acquirer performs a hostile bid, faces review and can account for minority payouts.
- Recovery player loses a firm and rebuilds without erasing the career record.
- Finance player experiences a bank liquidity squeeze and an insurance loss.
- Long-run player reaches five-year scoring, continues 30 years, saves and resumes.

Capture device, build, seed, steps, expected/actual, screenshots or recording, failures
and feedback. At least three independent testers complete a five-year route; this is a
human gate, not an AI-generated declaration that the experience is fun.
